Reading the map. Each dot is a deck, colored by its main cluster. t-SNE keeps neighborhoods: decks near each other share many cards, and a compact blob is a tight cluster. Distances between far-apart blobs aren't meaningful, so for "how close are these two strategies," use the table. Tightness is the median cosine similarity of a cluster's decks to its average deck. Similarity is the cosine between the two average decks.
